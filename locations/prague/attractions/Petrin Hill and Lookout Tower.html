Petřín Hill and Lookout Tower

  • Address: Petřínské sady, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://pid.cz/en/funicular/ (funicular)
  • Kind: viewpoint
  • Admission: partly free ($)
  • Hours: Funicular daily 07:00–22:15; tower hours not checked online
  • Time needed: 2–3 hours
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: if time allows
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

A wooded park on the hill above Malá Strana, topped by a mini Eiffel Tower built in 1891. It has some of the best views over the castle and the Old Town.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • The lookout tower: 299 steps (or a lift) to a viewing platform.
  • The funicular: it reopened on 22 September 2026 after two years of rebuilding, with new cars. It runs every 10 minutes.
  • The mirror maze next to the tower, and the rose garden.

Getting There

Tram to Újezd, then the funicular to the top. Or walk down afterwards to Strahov and Strahov Library; it’s a lovely downhill route.

Notes

  • Prices: the funicular costs 100 CZK. Not every PID public transport ticket is valid on it, so check before relying on a travel pass. The tower is 250 CZK, plus 150 CZK for the lift, according to a local guide.
  • The funicular is running on a trial basis after the reopening.
  • Funicular details come from the official PID site and Radio Prague (September 2026). The tower price comes from a late-2025 guide. The map coordinates are approximate.
