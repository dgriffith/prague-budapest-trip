Getting Around Prague

Prague’s metro, trams, buses, the Petřín funicular and ferries all run on one ticket system called PID. Tickets cover a set amount of time, not a set number of rides: you can change lines and vehicles as often as you like until the ticket runs out.

Our hotel, W Prague, is on Wenceslas Square, with Muzeum (metro lines A and C) at the top of the square and Můstek (lines A and B) at the bottom.

Tickets (2026 prices)

Fares went up on 1 January 2026. Tickets in the app are cheaper than paper ones.

30 minutes 36 CZK 39 CZK
90 minutes 46 CZK 50 CZK
24 hours 140 CZK 150 CZK
72 hours 340 CZK 350 CZK
  • Best buy for a few days of sightseeing: the 72-hour ticket. It covers metro, trams, buses, ferries and trains inside Prague.
  • Not covered by day tickets: the Airport Express (AE) bus and the vintage tram routes.
  • 24- and 72-hour tickets start when you validate them, so you get the full time from your first ride.
  • Children under 15 ride free. Seniors 60–65 pay half price and over-65s ride free (bring ID with your age on it).

How to pay

  • PID Lítačka app (cheapest): buy and activate before you get on. App tickets only become valid 2 minutes after purchase, so don’t buy one as the tram pulls in.
  • Paper tickets: from machines at metro stations and many tram stops (cards accepted). Stamp them in the yellow validator at the metro entrance or on board the tram or bus the first time you use them.
  • Tapping a bank card on a yellow validator buys one single ticket at full price each time. There is no daily cap, so it costs more than a day ticket if you ride a lot.

Rules worth knowing

  • There are no ticket gates. Plain-clothes inspectors check tickets often, and riding without a valid ticket gets an on-the-spot fine of around 1,000 CZK, more if paid later.
  • Keep your ticket (or phone) until you’re off the vehicle and out of the station.
  • Trams run all night on night routes; the metro runs from about 5am to midnight.

Airport

  • Cheapest: bus 59 from the airport to Nádraží Veleslavín (about 15 minutes), then metro line A to Muzeum or Můstek, right at the hotel. A 90-minute ticket (46 CZK in the app) covers the whole trip.
  • Simplest with luggage: the Airport Express (AE) bus to the main railway station (about 45 minutes, 200 CZK). From there it’s one stop on metro line C to Muzeum, or a short taxi ride. Day tickets don’t cover the AE.
  • Bus 100 runs to Zličín on metro line B, which also goes to Můstek.

Handy routes from the hotel

  • Old Town Square: walk (about 15 minutes) or metro A one stop to Staroměstská.
  • Prague Castle: metro A from Můstek to Malostranská, then walk up the Old Castle Stairs. Or take tram 22 uphill to the Pražský hrad stop and walk down through the castle instead of up.
  • Vinohrady: about 10 minutes on foot, or metro A one stop to Náměstí Míru.
