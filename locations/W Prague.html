W Prague

Our hotel for the Prague stay, situated right on Václavské náměstí (Wenceslas Square) — one of Prague’s main commercial and cultural hubs, and an ideal base for exploring the city.

Details

  • Address: Václavské náměstí 826/25, Prague 1
  • Neighbourhood: Nové Město (New Town), Prague 1
  • Chain: Marriott / W Hotels
  • Website: marriott.com

Location Notes

Wenceslas Square is within easy walking distance of:

  • The Old Town (Staré Město) and Old Town Square (~15 min walk)
  • The Jewish Quarter (Josefov) (~20 min walk)
  • Vinohrady neighbourhood (~10 min walk)
  • Múzeum metro station (Line A & C) — right on the doorstep

Nearby Restaurants

  • Kantýna — Czech butchery pub, very close
  • Cukrárna Myšák — historic pastry café, a short walk away
  • Café Imperial — Art Nouveau grand café, ~10 min walk
