Folkart Kézművesház

  • Address: Régi posta utca 12, Budapest 1052, Hungary
  • Website: Not checked online
  • Category: gifts & souvenirs (folk art)
  • Price: $$
  • Hours: Not checked online
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • The name means “Craftsman’s House”. Tripadvisor ranks it #31 of 459 shops in Budapest, and reviewers describe it as genuine handmade Hungarian folk art rather than tourist souvenirs.
  • One reviewer brought her mother, who knows a lot about hand-made versus machine-made textiles, and she was impressed by the quality. Reviewers also say the staff are knowledgeable and don’t push you to buy.

What to Look For

  • Needlework and embroidery, which a Rick Steves forum poster singled out. Look for regional styles like Matyó and Kalocsa.
  • Woven tablecloths, pottery and tiles.
  • Folk clothing.

Notes

In the inner city, just off Váci utca. It’s about 25 minutes’ walk from the Corinthia Budapest, or take the M2 to Deák Ferenc tér and walk 5–10 minutes. It’s near Herend Porcelain (József nádor tér) and Eventuell Gallery, so the three make an easy crafts route.

Other places for folk embroidery: the gift shop at the Hungarian National Museum is said to sell Matyó embroidery, and the Museum of Ethnography (Néprajzi Múzeum) is the place to see the real thing first.

The address and reviews come from Tripadvisor. I couldn’t check the hours, website, or that it’s still open. Map coordinates are approximate.
