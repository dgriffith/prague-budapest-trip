Szimpla Sunday Farmers’ Market

  • Address: Kazinczy utca 14, Budapest 1075 (District VII), Hungary
  • Website: Not checked online
  • Category: food & drink (market)
  • Price: $
  • Hours: Sundays 9:00–15:00 (2024 listing; check)
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • Every Sunday since 2012, the Szimpla Kert ruin bar turns into a farmers’ market. About 30–40 producers come, mostly from around Budapest.
  • It’s a chance to see the ruin bar in daylight, without the night-time crowds. There’s usually live music, and each week a different charity cooks food you can try for a donation.
  • Tripadvisor rates it 4.5 out of 5 from about 400 reviews.

What to Look For

  • Cheeses, cured meats, honey, breads and pastries.
  • Pickles, dips and spreads.
  • Go hungry. Many stalls sell tasting platters, so it can double as brunch.

Notes

About 10 minutes’ walk from the Corinthia Budapest, in the same streets as Massolit Books and Cafe and Printa.

Sundays only. Plan around it if your days in Budapest include a Sunday. One market guide calls it small and aimed at tourists, so think of it as a fun morning out rather than a big market.

The hours come from a 2024 events listing; Tripadvisor confirms it still runs every Sunday. Check the times before going. Map coordinates are approximate.
