Getting Around Budapest

Budapest’s metro, trams, buses and trolleybuses are run by BKK. Unlike Prague, a basic single ticket is for one ride, so for sightseeing a time-based ticket or travelcard is much easier.

Our hotel, Corinthia Budapest, is on the Grand Boulevard (Erzsébet körút). Trams 4 and 6 stop right outside, and the Blaha Lujza tér stop on metro line M2 is a few minutes’ walk away.

Tickets (2026 prices)

Ticket Price
Single ticket 500 HUF (700 HUF bought from the driver)
30-minute ticket, unlimited changes 600 HUF
90-minute ticket, unlimited changes 850 HUF
24-hour travelcard 2,750 HUF
72-hour travelcard 5,750 HUF
24-hour group travelcard (up to 5 people) 5,000 HUF
Budapest Card (transport plus attractions) from 17,990 HUF for 24 hours
  • Best buy: the 72-hour travelcard, or the 24-hour one on busy days. Six single tickets already cost more than a day card.
  • Not covered by travelcards: the 100E airport bus (separate ticket), the Castle Hill funicular and Danube boats.
  • Budapest Card: check it against the museums and baths we actually plan to visit before buying. Memento Park, for example, is free with it.

How to pay

  • BudapestGO app (recommended): BKK’s official app, in English. It plans routes with live departures and sells single tickets, time-based tickets and travelcards.
  • Tapping a bank card (Pay&GO): works at the metro. Tap the card or phone at the validator to buy and validate a single ticket in one step. It’s still being rolled out, so on trams and buses have a ticket ready.
  • Paper tickets: from the purple machines. Validate paper tickets in the validator on board or at the metro entrance before riding.

Rules worth knowing

  • Inspectors often stand at metro entrances and check every ticket. Have the app or ticket ready before going down the escalator.
  • A single ticket is for one trip on one vehicle. Changing between metro lines is allowed, but switching to a tram or bus needs a new ticket. A 30- or 90-minute ticket avoids this.
  • Keep your ticket until you leave the vehicle or station.

Airport

  • 100E Airport Express bus to Deák Ferenc tér in the centre, stopping at Kálvin tér and Astoria on the way. It takes about 35–45 minutes and runs around the clock.
  • Fare: 2,500 HUF per person (it went up from 2,200 HUF in October 2025). Regular travelcards and the Budapest Card don’t cover it, but you can tap a bank card on board.
  • From Astoria to the hotel: M2 metro two stops to Blaha Lujza tér, or a short taxi ride.

Handy routes from the hotel

  • Andrássy út, House of Terror, Heroes’ Square and City Park (Museum of Fine Arts, Széchenyi Baths): walk to Oktogon, then take the little yellow M1 metro under Andrássy út.
  • Lukács and Veli Bej baths: trams 4 or 6 over Margaret Bridge to the Buda side.
  • Great Synagogue and Central Market Hall: walk, or metro M2 to Astoria.
  • Castle Hill and Fishermen’s Bastion: M2 to the Batthyány tér or Széll Kálmán tér stops, then a short bus ride or the walk up. Or take the funicular from the Chain Bridge (separate ticket).
  • Scenic ride: tram 2 runs along the Pest bank of the Danube past Parliament. It’s one of the best views in the city for the price of a ticket.
