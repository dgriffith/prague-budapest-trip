Lukács Thermal Bath

  • Address: Frankel Leó út 25-29, 1023, Budapest, Hungary
  • Website: https://www.lukacsfurdo.hu
  • Style: other (19th-century spa courtyard)
  • Price: $$
  • Hours: Daily 7:00–19:00 (extended hours on some days)
  • Night bathing: no
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: if time allows
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

The locals’ bath. It’s an unflashy medicinal spa on the Buda side near Margaret Bridge, where pensioners swim their morning laps. Much quieter than Széchenyi or Rudas, and roughly half the price. Go here for a calm soak rather than a spectacle.

What to Expect

  • Thermal pools at different temperatures, an outdoor swimming pool and saunas.
  • A courtyard with plaques left by grateful patients thanking the waters for their cures.
  • A mostly local, older crowd. People come here for the medicinal water.

Notes

  • Price checked online: about 7,000 HUF (around €25). Entry is free with the Budapest Card.
  • Hours come from a travel guide updated in August 2026. Some days have extended hours; check the official site.
  • The address and website weren’t checked online.
  • Easy to reach from the hotel: trams 4 and 6 run along the Grand Boulevard right outside the Corinthia Budapest and cross Margaret Bridge to Buda. Get off just over the bridge and walk a few minutes north.
  • Veli Bej Bath is next door, so you could compare both on one trip.
