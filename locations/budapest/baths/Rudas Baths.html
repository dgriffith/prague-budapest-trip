Rudas Thermal Bath

  • Address: Döbrentei tér 9, 1013, Budapest, Hungary
  • Website: https://www.rudasfurdo.hu/en
  • Style: ottoman
  • Price: $$$
  • Hours: Pools daily 6:00–20:00; wellness and sauna 8:00–22:00; night bathing Fri–Sat 22:00–03:00
  • Night bathing: yes
  • Booking: recommended
  • Priority: must-see
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

A working 16th-century Turkish bath at the foot of Gellért Hill, by the Elisabeth Bridge. The heart of it is an octagonal pool under an Ottoman dome, with light coming through small coloured-glass holes in the roof. With Gellért and Király both closed, this is the best place to get the Ottoman bath experience. It’s the counterpoint to Széchenyi’s grand palace.

What to Expect

  • The Ottoman bath hall: the central octagonal pool and smaller pools around it at different temperatures.
  • A rooftop panorama pool, with views across the Danube to Pest.
  • A wellness section with a swimming pool, saunas and steam rooms.
  • Night bathing on Friday and Saturday nights. The rooftop pool and saunas are closed then.

Notes

  • Prices checked on the official 2026 price list: a daily all-zone ticket is 12,000 HUF Monday to Thursday, 15,000 HUF Friday to Sunday, and 16,000 HUF on holidays and in high season.
  • Night-bathing tickets (about 15,000 HUF) are sold online only.
  • No visitors under 14.
  • Bring your own towel, slippers and swimsuit. On-site they cost a lot (a towel is about 6,600 HUF).
  • On the Buda side, across the river. About 15 minutes by taxi from the Corinthia Budapest. Rudas Bistro on site has Danube views if you want lunch after.
