Danube Bend – Visegrád and Esztergom

  • Address: Szent István tér 1, 2500, Esztergom, Hungary (the Basilica)
  • Website: https://bazilika-esztergom.hu/en/
  • Priority: must-see (the most scenic day trip)
  • Time needed: a full day

About

The Danube Bend is where the river makes a sharp turn south, 40–50 km north of Budapest, between wooded hills. Two historic towns sit on it:

  • Visegrád: a medieval hilltop citadel with sweeping views of the bend, and the remains of a royal palace below.
  • Esztergom: Hungary’s old royal capital and home of the Esztergom Basilica, the largest church in Hungary. You can climb about 400 steps to the dome for a view across the river into Slovakia.

Szentendre is on the same stretch of river, so the three are often done together on organised tours. Doing all three yourself in one day is a rush; pick two, or see Szentendre on a separate day.

Getting There

  • Organised tours: full-day tours from Budapest cover Esztergom, Visegrád and Szentendre with hotel pickup. The easiest option if you don’t want to piece together connections.
  • Boat: Mahart boats run from about April to September. In summer 2026 they were suspended for weeks because of very low water, so check the timetable close to the date.
  • Train and bus: possible on your own, but the connections weren’t checked online. Ask for the latest routes in the BKK/MÁV apps.

Esztergom Basilica Hours

  • The main church (liturgical space) is open 8:00–17:00 in the winter period on the official site.
  • The crypt, treasury and dome have their own hours and close for part of the winter (12 January–27 February in 2026). From late February the dome lookout opens 9:00–16:00.
  • Hours change by season, so check the official site for your dates.

Notes

  • Checked online: the Basilica’s official opening-hours page, the boat season and the 2026 low-water suspension.
  • Not checked online: the Visegrád citadel’s hours and prices, and train and bus connections. The map coordinates are approximate.
