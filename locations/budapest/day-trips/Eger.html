Eger

  • Address: Eger, Hungary (about 2 hours north-east of Budapest)
  • Website: Not checked online
  • Priority: if time allows (best for wine lovers)
  • Time needed: a long full day, or one night

About

A handsome baroque town and one of Hungary’s best-known wine regions. The main draw is the Valley of the Beautiful Women (Szépasszony-völgy): a horseshoe of small wine cellars dug into the hillside, where you wander from cellar to cellar tasting local wine.

Getting There

  • Train from Budapest-Keleti station: about 2 hours (the fastest trains take 1 hour 52 minutes), with trains every few hours. Tickets are cheap, from about $8.
  • From Eger station, a small white tourist train goes to the wine valley. A traveller review says it leaves from the corner of Klapka György and Egészségház streets.
  • Organised wine tours from Budapest also run, but private ones are expensive.

What to See and Drink

Not checked online — from general knowledge, so confirm before going:

  • Egri Bikavér (“Bull’s Blood”), the famous local red blend.
  • Eger Castle, known for a famous 1552 siege against the Ottomans.
  • A minaret left from the Ottoman period, one of the most northerly in Europe.
  • The old-town squares and churches.

Notes

  • The train journey and wine valley details were checked online. The castle, minaret and wine details weren’t. The map coordinates are approximate.
  • With a 4-hour round trip, this is worth considering as an overnight stay rather than a day trip.
  • If you want a taste of Hungarian wine without the trip, Borkonyha Winekitchen in Budapest has a big Hungarian wine list.
