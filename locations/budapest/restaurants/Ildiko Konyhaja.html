Ildikó Konyhája

  • Address: Fő utca 8, Budapest 1011 (District I, Buda side, below Castle Hill)
  • Website: Not checked online
  • Cuisine: Traditional Hungarian
  • Price: $$
  • Reservation: no
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

A cosy, family-style Hungarian restaurant (“Ildikó’s kitchen”) at the foot of Castle Hill. Reviewers praise the warm welcome and generous portions. A food guide picked its palacsinta as a highlight.

What to Order

  • Palacsinta: the main reason it’s on this list.
  • Hungarian classics for the main course.

Notes

  • Closed on Sundays. Opens 11:30 Monday to Saturday. Sources disagree on closing time (17:00 or 20:00), so treat it as a lunch place unless confirmed.
  • A good lunch stop on a Castle Hill day, with the National Gallery and Budapest History Museum. It’s just across the Chain Bridge from Pest.
