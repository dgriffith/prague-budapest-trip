Hospital in the Rock (Sziklakórház)

  • Address: Lovas út 4/c, Castle Hill, 1012, Budapest, Hungary
  • Website: https://www.sziklakorhaz.eu/en
  • Focus: WWII emergency hospital and Cold War nuclear bunker in the caves under Buda Castle
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: Daily, guided tours only (roughly hourly). Check website
  • Booking: recommended
  • Priority: if time allows
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

A secret hospital built into the cave system under Castle Hill. It treated wounded soldiers and civilians during the 1944–45 siege of Budapest and again during the 1956 uprising. It was later expanded into a nuclear bunker. The tour goes through wards and operating rooms set up with wax figures and original equipment. It’s unusual and memorable.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • The original operating theatres
  • The Cold War nuclear bunker section

Notes

  • Not checked online: tour times and prices weren’t looked up.
  • Guided tours only, about 1 hour. It’s cool underground, so bring a layer.
  • Pair it with the Hungarian National Gallery and a walk around the Castle District.
