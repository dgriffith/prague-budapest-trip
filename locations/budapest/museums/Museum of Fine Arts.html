Museum of Fine Arts (Szépművészeti Múzeum)

  • Address: Dózsa György út 41, Heroes’ Square, 1146, Budapest, Hungary
  • Website: https://www.szepmuveszeti.hu/en
  • Focus: European Old Masters (especially the Spanish collection), Egyptian and classical antiquities
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, last entry 17:00 (closed Mondays)
  • Booking: recommended
  • Priority: must-see
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

Budapest’s main art museum. Franz Joseph opened it in 1906, and it gets over half a million visits a year. Its Spanish collection is one of the best outside Spain. It sits on Heroes’ Square at the top of Andrássy, so it pairs easily with City Park, the Museum of Ethnography (Néprajzi Múzeum) and the House of Music Hungary (Magyar Zene Háza).

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • Spanish Old Masters: El Greco, Velázquez, Goya
  • The restored Romanesque Hall
  • Egyptian and Greco-Roman collections

Notes

  • Reported adult ticket is about 5,800 HUF. Combined tickets with temporary exhibitions cost more (one booking site lists about $25).
  • On the second Friday of each month, it reportedly opens at noon and stays open until 19:30.
  • Plan on 2 hours to half a day.
  • About 25 minutes’ walk from the hotel along Andrássy, or take the M1 (yellow line) to Hősök tere.
