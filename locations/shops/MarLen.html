MarLen

  • Address: Karolíny Světlé 299/12, 110 00 Staré Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.marlen.cz
  • Category: other (fabric and yarn)
  • Price: —
  • Hours: Mon 9:00–18:00 (other days not confirmed)
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • A long-standing Czech supplier of fabric by the metre and good-quality knitting yarn, with shops in Prague and Brno.
  • Covers both interests in one stop: fabric, yarn, and supplies for knitting and crochet.
  • Wide range of materials, from silks and hand-woven fabrics to wool, linen, viscose and cashmere, plus lace, brocade, satin and velvet.

What to Look For

  • Fabrics they import or have made themselves, which you won’t find elsewhere.
  • Their yarn selection, since dedicated yarn shops in central Prague are rare.

Notes

In the Old Town near the river, about a 15-minute walk from the W Prague. Easy to combine with a walk to Charles Bridge.
