Filium

  • Address: Palladium Shopping Centre, náměstí Republiky 1, 110 00 Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.filium.cz
  • Category: other (yarn and haberdashery)
  • Price: $$
  • Hours: —
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • One of the best-stocked haberdashery and knitting shops in central Prague.
  • Yarns from Rowan, Lang Yarns and Austermann, plus Gütermann thread, DMC embroidery floss, Dill buttons and needles from Addi, KnitPro and Prym.

What to Look For

  • Dill buttons, which are hard to find in the US.
  • Lang and Austermann yarns.

Notes

  • It’s on the lowest level of Palladium, in the passage by the Náměstí Republiky metro entrance. That’s about a 10-minute walk from the W Prague.
  • A Prague knitter on the Rick Steves forum says it’s fine for everyday yarn but more like a small fabric shop than a boutique yarn store, and that staff may not speak English.
