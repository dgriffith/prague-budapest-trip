Luxor Palace of Books (Palác knih Luxor)

  • Address: Václavské náměstí 41, 110 00 Nové Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.luxor.cz
  • Category: books
  • Price: $$
  • Hours: —
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • One of Prague’s biggest general bookshops, spread over several floors on Wenceslas Square.
  • Has an English-language section alongside Czech titles, maps, stationery and gifts.

What to Look For

  • Illustrated Czech children’s books (Krtek, the Little Mole, and similar), which make good gifts.
  • Prague guidebooks and maps.

Notes

A couple of doors from the W Prague on Wenceslas Square.
