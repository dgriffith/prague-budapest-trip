Yarn Queen Prague

  • Address: Řipská 2386/11, 130 00 Vinohrady, Praha 3, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.vlna-prize.cz
  • Category: other (yarn)
  • Price: —
  • Hours: —
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • Probably Prague’s best dedicated yarn shop, and one of the few independent ones left in the city.
  • Run by its owner, Šárka Mošová, and full of yarns, tools and accessories for beginners and experienced knitters.
  • Listed on Ravelry as a local yarn shop, and well reviewed.

What to Look For

  • Yarns from Czech and other European dyers.
  • Their e-shop, if you want more than fits in a suitcase.

Notes

In Vinohrady, near the Perunova tram stop. It’s farther from the W Prague than the other shops, so it’s easiest by tram. Check the hours on their website before you go.
