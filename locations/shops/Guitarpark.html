Guitarpark

  • Address: Jungmannovo náměstí 754/17, 110 00 Nové Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: —
  • Category: other (musical instruments)
  • Price: —
  • Hours: —
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • The closest proper instrument shop to the hotel, just off the lower end of Wenceslas Square.
  • Sells stringed and keyboard instruments, plus accessories.
  • Does repairs and setups: fret work, intonation and tremolo adjustment, neck straightening.

What to Look For

  • Strings and small accessories that are easy to carry home.
  • Czech-made guitars, if they stock any.

Notes

A few minutes’ walk from the W Prague, at the bottom of Wenceslas Square. For a much bigger selection, Muziker has a large store at Radlická 3201/14 in Smíchov (Anděl), a short metro ride away.
