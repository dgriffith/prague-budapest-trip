The Globe Bookstore and Café

  • Address: Pštrossova 1925/6, 110 00 Nové Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: —
  • Category: books
  • Price: $$
  • Hours:
    • Mon–Thu 10:00–23:00
    • Fri 10:00–24:00
    • Sat 9:30–24:00
    • Sun 9:30–23:00
  • Visited: planned

Why Visit

  • Founded in 1993 as Prague’s first English-language bookshop, and still the centre of the city’s English-speaking literary scene.
  • Over 10,000 titles across fiction, non-fiction, poetry and children’s books.
  • A café and bar in the same space that stays open late, with events and readings.

What to Look For

  • Their staff picks and the Czech-authors-in-translation shelf.
  • The event calendar, to see if there’s a reading during the stay.

Notes

About a 15-minute walk from the W Prague, near the National Theatre. The kitchen closes at 22:00.
