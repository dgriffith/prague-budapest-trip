La Degustation Bohême Bourgeoise

  • Address: Haštalská 18, 110 00 Staré Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.ladegustation.cz
  • Cuisine: Czech, Fine Dining, Tasting Menu
  • Price: $$$$
  • Reservation: yes
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

Prague’s most storied Michelin-starred restaurant, celebrated for its long tasting menus rooted in traditional Bohemian cuisine reimagined through a fine-dining lens. Featured in Anthony Bourdain’s work. Located in Staré Město (Old Town), about 15 minutes’ walk from the W Prague.

Why Go

  • 1 Michelin Star
  • Multi-course tasting menus showcasing elevated Czech culinary heritage
  • Exceptional wine list with Moravian wines
  • Special occasion / splurge dinner

Notes

Reservations are essential and should be made well in advance. This is the splurge dinner of the trip.
