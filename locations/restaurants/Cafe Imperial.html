Café Imperial

  • Address: Na Poříčí 1072/15, 110 00 Petrská čtvrť, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.cafeimperial.cz
  • Cuisine: Czech, Grand Café
  • Price: $$$
  • Reservation: recommended
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

One of Prague’s most spectacular dining rooms — a stunning 1914 Art Nouveau space tiled floor-to-ceiling with geometric ceramic relief. Operated by celebrity chef Zdeněk Pohlreich since 2007. A short walk from the W Prague.

Why Go

  • The room itself is worth the trip — Franz Kafka was a regular in spaces like this
  • Excellent Czech classics: schnitzel, kulajda (creamy dill mushroom soup), roast goose (seasonal)
  • Roast goose served around St. Martin’s Day (November) — a Czech tradition
  • Upscale but not stuffy

Notes

Make a reservation, especially for dinner. The lunch menu is more affordable. Look out for the goose special if visiting in autumn.
