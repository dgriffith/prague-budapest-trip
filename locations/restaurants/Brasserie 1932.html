Brasserie 1932

  • Address: Štěpánská 623/40, 110 00 Nové Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.almanachotels.com/praguex/food-drinks/brasserie-1932
  • Cuisine: French, Modern Czech
  • Price: $$$$
  • Reservation: recommended
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

The restaurant of the Almanac X Alcron hotel. It’s the new name of the hotel’s famous Alcron restaurant, which has been cooking in this building since 1932. It does French cooking with a Czech twist in a 1930s-style dining room, and is rated 4.7 on Tripadvisor.

What to Order

  • Reviewers mention a guinea fowl pasta and the pavlova
  • There’s also a plant-forward menu that gets good reviews

Notes

Just off Wenceslas Square, a few minutes’ walk from the W Prague. Open from noon. Booking is through SevenRooms.
