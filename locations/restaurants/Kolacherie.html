Kolacherie

  • Address: Celetná 589/27, 110 00 Staré Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.kolacherie.cz
  • Cuisine: Czech Pastry, Bakery, Café
  • Price: $
  • Reservation: no
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

A fun, modern bakery in Prague’s Old Town specializing in koláče — traditional Czech pastries with a fun Texas twist. Czech immigrants brought koláče to Texas, where the pastry evolved, and Kolacherie brings that evolution back to Bohemia. Great grab-and-go option while sightseeing.

Why Go

  • Traditional koláče: poppy seed, cream cheese, plum — made with local ingredients and a family recipe
  • Unexpected Texas-style variants: sausage and jalapeño
  • Located on Celetná Street — perfect for a snack while walking from Old Town Square
  • Quick, cheap, and delicious

Notes

Ideal for a mid-morning or afternoon snack while exploring Old Town. No reservations, walk-in only.
