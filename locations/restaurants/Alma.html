Alma

  • Address: V Jirchářích 150/8, 110 00 Nové Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://almaprague.cz
  • Cuisine: Contemporary, Modern Czech
  • Price: $$
  • Reservation: recommended
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

A contemporary restaurant with an open kitchen and bar, holding a Michelin Bib Gourmand. It works with local suppliers, such as KRO Bakery in Vršovice, and has a strong wine list that includes wines made just for Alma.

What to Order

  • Dry-aged beef
  • The vegetarian dishes; there are vegan and gluten-free options too
  • Ask the sommelier for a glass to match each course

Notes

In Nové Město near the National Theatre, roughly a 15-minute walk from the W Prague. Open late, so it works for a late dinner or drinks.
