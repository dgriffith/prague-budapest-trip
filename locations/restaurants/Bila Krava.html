Bílá Kráva

  • Address: Rubešova 10, 120 00 Vinohrady, Praha 2, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: —
  • Cuisine: French, Steakhouse, Czech
  • Price: $$$
  • Reservation: recommended
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

A French countryside-style steak restaurant just behind the National Museum, under a wood-beamed ceiling. It was one of the first places in Prague to cook with Charolais beef. The beef is aged and comes from Czech farmers. The name means “White Cow”.

What to Order

  • Aged Charolais steaks: tenderloin and onglet both get good reviews
  • Potato gratin as a side, which reviewers call out on its own
  • French classics such as boeuf bourguignon and snails

Notes

About a 10-minute walk from the W Prague, up past the top of Wenceslas Square and behind the National Museum. Book ahead for dinner.
