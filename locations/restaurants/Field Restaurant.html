Field Restaurant

  • Address: U Milosrdných 12, 110 00 Staré Město, Praha 1, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.fieldrestaurant.cz
  • Cuisine: Czech, Modern European, Michelin Star
  • Price: $$$$
  • Reservation: yes
  • Rating: —
  • Visited: planned

Overview

Prague’s second Michelin-starred restaurant — minimalist, farm-to-table in spirit, and deeply committed to Czech ingredients and flavors. Chef Radek Kašpárek’s tasting menus evoke the Czech landscape with striking modern technique. Located in Staré Město, ~15 minutes from the W Prague.

Why Go

  • 1 Michelin Star
  • Seasonal tasting menus: 6-course and 10-course options
  • Standout dishes: tomato/yogurt/basil amuse, foie gras, veal tenderloin, sturgeon
  • Tableside sauce pours and dry ice presentations — theatrical and memorable
  • Affordable weekday lunch menu — great value entry point

Notes

Book well in advance for dinner tasting menus. The lunch menu is a smart budget-friendly alternative to experience the kitchen.
