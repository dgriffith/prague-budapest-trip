Museum of Communism

  • Address: V Celnici 1031/4, Nové Město, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://muzeumkomunismu.cz
  • Focus: Everyday life, politics, and repression in Communist Czechoslovakia (1948–1989)
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: {{hours}}
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: must-see
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

A compact, well-told look at four decades of Communist rule — the dream, the reality, and the nightmare — ending with the 1989 Velvet Revolution.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • Recreated period shop, classroom, and interrogation room
  • Footage from the 1968 invasion and 1989 protests
