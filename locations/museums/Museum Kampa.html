Museum Kampa

  • Address: U Sovových mlýnů 2, Malá Strana, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.museumkampa.cz
  • Focus: Central European modern art — František Kupka, Otto Gutfreund and the Jan & Meda Mládek Collection
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: Daily 10:00–18:00
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: if time allows
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

The museum is in the old Sova Mills on Kampa Island, right on the Vltava. It holds the Mládek collection: works by Kupka, a pioneer of abstract art, and the Cubist sculptor Gutfreund, plus art made in the Eastern Bloc under communism. The riverside setting is half the appeal.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • Kupka’s abstract paintings
  • Gutfreund’s Cubist sculpture
  • Outdoor sculptures along the river (including David Černý’s giant crawling babies nearby on Kampa)

Notes

  • Tram 12, 15, 20 or 22 to Hellichova.
  • Easy to pair with a walk across Charles Bridge and around Malá Strana.
