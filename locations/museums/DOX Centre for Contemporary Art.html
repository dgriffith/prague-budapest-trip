DOX Centre for Contemporary Art

  • Address: Poupětova 1, Holešovice, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.dox.cz/en
  • Focus: Contemporary art, architecture and design
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: Mon & Sat–Sun 10:00–18:00; Wed & Fri 11:00–19:00; Thu 11:00–21:00; closed Tue
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: must-see
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

The Czech Republic’s largest independent contemporary art centre. It opened in 2008 in a converted factory in Holešovice and runs bold, often political exhibitions mixing art, literature, performance and architecture.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • The Gulliver Airship, a huge wood-and-steel zeppelin sitting on the roof, used as a reading and event space
  • Rotating headline exhibitions
  • The design shop and bookstore

Notes

  • About 10 minutes from the centre. Easy to pair with the National Technical Museum and Veletržní palác (National Gallery), which are also in Holešovice.
