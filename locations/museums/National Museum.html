National Museum

  • Address: Václavské náměstí 68, Nové Město, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.nm.cz/en
  • Focus: Czech natural history, history, and culture; the country’s largest museum
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: Daily 10:00–18:00
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: if time allows
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

The grand Neo-Renaissance building at the top of Wenceslas Square is worth seeing for its architecture alone. The museum complex covers Czech history and natural history over two linked buildings.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • The Pantheon under the main dome
  • The grand staircase and the views down Wenceslas Square
  • Natural history halls, including a whale skeleton
  • The underground passage to the New Building (the former Parliament / Radio Free Europe building)

Notes

  • Metro A or C to Muzeum.
  • Easy to pair with the Museum of Communism, which is a short walk away.
