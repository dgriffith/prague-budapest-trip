Museum of Alchemists and Magicians of Old Prague

  • Address: Jánský vršek 8, Malá Strana, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: {{website}}
  • Focus: Alchemy and magic in Rudolfine Prague (Edward Kelley, John Dee, Rudolf II)
  • Admission: paid ($)
  • Hours: Daily 10:00–20:00
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: optional
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

A small, quirky and slightly kitschy museum in a house said to have belonged to the alchemist Edward Kelley. It’s fun if you like Prague’s occult legends.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • The recreated alchemist’s laboratory
  • The tower room, where legend says Kelley worked

Notes

  • Reviews are mixed (around 3.8★). It’s more an attraction than a serious museum.
  • It’s on the way up to the castle from Malá Strana.
