Museum of Czech Cubism

  • Address: House at the Black Madonna, Ovocný trh 19, Staré Město, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.upm.cz/czech-cubism/
  • Focus: Czech Cubism in furniture, applied art and architecture (Museum of Decorative Arts)
  • Admission: paid ($)
  • Hours: Tue 10:00–20:00; Wed–Sun 10:00–18:00; closed Mon
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: if time allows
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

Prague is the only city where Cubism became a full architectural style. This museum is inside the first Cubist building, the House at the Black Madonna, built in 1911–12 by Josef Gočár as a department store. The building is the main exhibit.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • The building itself: the Cubist façade and staircase
  • Whole suites of Cubist furniture and ceramics
  • Grand Café Orient on the first floor, the world’s only Cubist café
  • Modernista shop on the ground floor, selling replicas of Cubist ceramics

Notes

  • Full ticket 150 CZK, concession 80 CZK (from the museum’s site).
  • A few minutes’ walk from the Old Town Square and the Powder Tower.
