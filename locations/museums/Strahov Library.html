Strahov Library

  • Address: Strahovské nádvoří 1, Hradčany, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://www.strahovskyklaster.cz
  • Focus: Baroque monastic library with the Theological and Philosophical Halls
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: Daily 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30)
  • Booking: no
  • Priority: must-see
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

One of the most beautiful historic libraries in the world. It belongs to the Strahov Monastery, holds around 200,000 volumes and has spectacular frescoed halls.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • Theological Hall: stucco ceilings and antique globes
  • Philosophical Hall: floor-to-ceiling walnut shelves and a huge ceiling fresco
  • Cabinet of curiosities in the corridor

Notes

  • You view the halls from the doorways and can’t walk inside them.
  • Basic ticket is about 340 CZK.
  • Some sources mention a 12:00–13:00 lunch closure, so check before you go.
  • It’s a short walk uphill from Prague Castle, so it fits well into a castle day.
