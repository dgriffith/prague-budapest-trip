Mucha Museum

  • Address: Savarin Palace, Na Příkopě 10, Nové Město, Prague, Czech Republic
  • Website: https://mucha.eu/en
  • Focus: Life and work of Art Nouveau master Alphonse Mucha (1860–1939)
  • Admission: paid ($$)
  • Hours: Daily 10:00–18:00 (closed Dec 24)
  • Booking: recommended
  • Priority: must-see
  • Visited: planned

Why Go

The official Mucha Foundation museum, which opened in 2025 in the restored Baroque Savarin Palace. It goes well beyond the famous posters to show paintings, drawings, photographs and works that have never been exhibited before.

Highlights / Must-Sees

  • Iconic Art Nouveau posters (Sarah Bernhardt series, The Seasons)
  • Lesser-known paintings and Mucha’s own photography
  • The Baroque palace interiors themselves

Notes

  • Adult ticket is about 350 CZK.
  • Don’t mix it up with the older Mucha Museum in Kounice Palace (Panská 7). This one is the museum backed by the Mucha family.
